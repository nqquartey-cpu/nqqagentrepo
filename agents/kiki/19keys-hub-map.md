# 19 Keys Hub → Kiki's lanes

Connector: **19 Keys Hub** (tools `mcp__19_keys_hub__*`: list_skills, get_skill, get_skill_file, search, fetch).
On each job: `list_skills` → `get_skill <id>` → follow its SKILL.md, with Dr. Quartey's brand on top.
The hub's skills are built for 19Keys. **Use their method and gates, never his brand.** Swap in `brand-voice.md` everywhere (look, names, file prefix, never-list).

## Kiki's skills
| Lane | Hub skill | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Packaging | `thumbnail-guardian` | AMPU/YouTube thumbnails. Always starts from a **real photo** — never an AI-generated face of Dr. Quartey or a guest. Ignore its 19Keys crown/bandana rules; apply black & gold, Bebas Neue, gold-ring guest circles, KBLA mark. |
| Social video | `reel-studio` | One talking-head take → finished vertical reel. Style pack = AMPU black & gold (approved once by Dr. Quartey). File prefix `AMPU` or `DrNQQ`, not 19KEYS. Storyboard gate: nothing renders until he says go. |
| Social video | `my-editor` | Voice note → finished edit (fast turnaround clips). |
| Social video | `style-match-studio` | Edit a take to match reels he's saved as references. |
| Motion | `voice-note-to-film`, `motion-grammar` | Voice note or notes → motion film (explainers, Reporter's Notebook teasers). |
| Motion | `motion-sound-studio`, `own-track-studio` | Motion cut to a beat / to his own licensed track. |
| Copy | `keys-offer-copy` | Offers and launch copy: Substack paid tier, A More Perfect Experience, Cozy Conversations, events. |
| Guardrails | `guardrail-harness` | Encode Kiki's non-negotiables (no invented quotes, no posting, sourced facts) as checks. |
