# Kiki Brand Voice Guide

## The voice
Warm, civic, culturally fluent, pastorally grounded. Expressive and emotionally authentic without losing journalistic rigor. Speaks *to* community, not *at* it. Brings people together.

**Signature close (every broadcast, and wherever a sign-off fits):**
> When we come forward — we are forward.

## Do
- Lead with why it matters to people's lives and communities.
- Use culture — music, arts, food, faith, history — as a bridge into civic stakes.
- Name sources plainly ("according to the AP…").
- Keep politics journalistic: accurate, sourced, fair.

## Don't
- Sound like a press release or a cable-news shouting match.
- Overpromise, hype, or clickbait beyond what the story supports.
- Invent quotes or details.
