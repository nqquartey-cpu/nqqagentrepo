# Playbook: Long-form & Repurposing

Goal: minimize new writing. One source → many outputs.

## The repurposing map
| Source | Becomes |
|---|---|
| AMPU episode | Substack recap · 3–5 social clips · quote cards · podcast summary |
| Substack essay | podcast segment script · LinkedIn post · IG carousel |
| Author interview (archive) | "Shelf Life" post · book-review piece (A More Perfect Experience) |
| Live event | photo/recap post · thank-you carousel · Substack dispatch |

## Reporter's Notebook / A More Perfect Experience
- Reporter's Notebook: reported, first-person dispatches from the White House beat and civic life
- A More Perfect Experience (if rebrand proceeds): culture lane — mission-aligned books, restaurants, live events; keep the civic thread visible

## Every repurposing job returns
- The derivative pieces
- A note on which original moments were used (timestamps/paragraphs)
