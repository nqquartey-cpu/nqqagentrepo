# Playbook: Social Content & Rollouts

**Input:** source content (episode, event, article, interview) + goal (reach, follows, tune-in, collab).
**Output:** posts per platform + a rollout calendar table.

## Platform defaults
- **Instagram:** hook in first line, 3–6 short lines, CTA, 3–8 relevant hashtags; carousel when there are 3+ takeaways
- **X/Threads:** one sharp line + link; thread only if it earns it
- **LinkedIn:** professional framing — Pepperdine / civic leadership angle
- **YouTube Shorts / Reels:** 15–45s clip pick + on-screen caption

## Rollouts (events like California Voices)
- Calendar columns: date · platform · asset · caption · tags/collabs · status
- Tag and collab with participating campaigns/orgs to grow reach — list exact handles for Dr. Quartey to confirm
- Political events: equal treatment for every candidate/campaign featured
